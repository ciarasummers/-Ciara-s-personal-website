THE GIRL BEHIND THE WEBSITE

I'm not really

I'm Jessica — an Agricultural Engineering student at the University of Ghana, but honestly, engineering is only one part of me.

I'm the kind of person who gets interested in a little bit of everything. I love creating content, skincare, movies, books, baking, going out, spending time with people I love and learning things simply because I'm curious.

I'm still figuring out exactly where all these interests will take me, and I'm okay with that. For now, I'm enjoying becoming the woman I want to be.

04　ENGINEERING, ACTUALLY
THE TECHNICAL CHAPTER

Oh, and I
study engineering.

I'm studying Agricultural Engineering at the University of Ghana. I'm especially interested in practical technology, smart agriculture, automation and ideas that can make agricultural systems work better.

01🌾

Project Agritec

A proposed academic design for a 100,000-bushel grain storage and handling facility, covering storage, receiving, cleaning, drying, conveying and monitoring.

farm structures · storage · design
02📡

Smart Agriculture

Exploring IoT, sensors, automation and practical technology for agriculture — especially ideas useful in African farming contexts.

IoT · sensors · smart agritech
